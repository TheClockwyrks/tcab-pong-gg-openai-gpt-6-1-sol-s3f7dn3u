
# Carom

Two paddles. One ball. A court that never gives you the same angle twice. Carom is a fast, top-down duel where every return is a chance to change the shape of the rally. Take on the machine in Solo, or challenge a friend in local Versus.

The opening serve gives you a moment to find your footing. Meet the ball at the center of your paddle for a clean return, or catch it near an edge to send it climbing toward a wall. Every paddle hit adds speed, turning a quiet exchange into a test of timing and nerve.

Move as you strike and the ball bends away with spin. The two gold bars sway and rotate through the middle of the court: use them to steal an unexpected bank shot, or watch a seemingly safe return come back at a completely new angle. The fading trail shows the curve you just carved.

A point goes to the player who sends the ball beyond the opponent's edge. First to eleven wins, but you must lead by two—so a close match can keep stretching, one desperate save and one daring angle at a time.
